# Center PTA SEO Sprint - 2026-04-27

## Current Position

Center for Psychological Treatment and Assessment already has the core SEO assets we need:

- Service pages exist for immigration evaluations, psychological assessment, pre-surgical evaluations, women's mental health, prenatal therapy, postpartum therapy, weight loss counseling, bariatric counseling, divorce counseling, and self-compassion therapy.
- The GBP is verified, but externally weak: 5.0 rating with 1 Google review, managed profile visible, and low posting/review depth.
- The strongest immediate gap is not page existence. It is specificity, competitive framing, internal linking, schema depth, and GBP activity.

## Strategic Direction

Do not make one topic sacred. Use a portfolio:

1. Immigration psychological evaluations
2. Pre-surgical and bariatric psychological evaluations
3. Women's mental health and reproductive/postpartum support
4. Divorce and relationship transition support
5. Multilingual/local trust: English, Serbian, Spanish in Schaumburg and Illinois telehealth

The revenue-first wedge is evaluations. The brand wedge is women's mental health and self-compassion. The local trust wedge is Schaumburg plus multilingual care.

### Priority 5: Review Plan

Current GBP has only 1 Google review. Month 1 target should be 5-10 real reviews, not vanity volume.

Plan:

- Ask eligible satisfied clients/referral partners where ethically appropriate.
- Use short neutral request language.
- Do not incentivize reviews.
- Do not imply clients should disclose diagnosis, treatment, immigration status, or protected health details.
- Add review request to post-session/admin workflow only where clinically appropriate.
